- (decision) Positioning: open-source product for third parties, driven by personal dogfooding (Gaspar's own daily use is the continuous swappability test)
- (decision) Kernel: custom plugin kernel in TypeScript (not Cordis, not Rust) - contract ownership is the product; Cordis/dsh studied as reference only
- (decision) v1 surface: SDK/headless first; terminal shell (teraz/terax style) deferred as a future plugin consumer
- (decision) v1 swappable contracts: ExecutorAdapter, MemoryProvider, ToolProvider, ProjectionTarget, SkillSource. Fixed kernel core: config resolution/scopes, event bus, plugin lifecycle
- (event) Prior art researched: deepseek-harness (Cordis, everything-is-a-plugin), terax-ai (Tauri terminal ADE), gentle-ai stack (partial shared MCPs/skills), runcell (SDK primitives), orca (parallel/SSH worktrees, usage tracking -> roadmap), agtx/sweteam/orch (CLI orchestrators)
- (decision) Problem framing confirmed by Gaspar: all three pains are core and inseparable (tool fragmentation, agent lock-in, device non-portability); agent lock-in is why panda exists, portability is its ultimate proof
- (decision) Audience: anyone who wants the same setup as the author. Panda is a platform - the SDK/kernel lets others build whatever they want on top; author's terminal is just its first consumer. Target segments: multi-agent devs, teams standardizing AI tooling, harness builders on the kernel, startups building products on it, indie hackers, independent developers
- (decision) Success criteria tiered per Gaspar: v1 exit threshold = S1-S4 + 30-day full dogfooding (required); long-term platform signals (external contract implementor, third-party kernel product, community adoption) are directional and measured-not-promised - may take years, never release gates
- (decision) Vision locked: 'Rent the agents. Own the environment.' - ownership inversion as panda's unique identity; environment is the durable asset, agents are interchangeable visitors. Hardened prose: declarative, no hedging, no coordination/kernel/platform claims that competitors could sign
- (event) Finalize: memlog audit done (9/9 entries mapped to brief or addendum); editorial pass applied 9 findings (structure 3, prose 6) via bmad-review lenses; finding about Problem parenthetical resolved by KEEPING it (pain evidence)
